Too Fat To Fly: Gravity Always Wins

← Back to main page

02.11.2026

Meet our hero: a small orange bug with big dreams, tiny wings and an even bigger appetite. Every other bug in town zips through the sky. Ours? Ours takes the stairs. Or, more precisely, the crates, the bricks, the planks and whatever else happens to be lying around the farm.

Too Fat To Fly (2F2F) is a 2D platformer for PC about doing things the hard way. You jump, you climb, you squeeze between platforms that were clearly built with slimmer bugs in mind, and you collect batteries because, honestly, someone has to. Along the way you'll meet the locals, who have plenty of opinions and very little sympathy. Some will help. Some will just talk. Randall definitely talks.

We'd love to tell you there is a deep message about self-acceptance here. There might be. Mostly, though, there is gravity, and gravity is undefeated.

The art is hand-drawn, the autumn never ends and the leaves never stop falling, much like our hero after a badly timed jump.

2F2F is in development right now. We're feeding it content one level at a time. It keeps asking for snacks.
