STARRUST: Rule the Galaxy on Your Coffee Break

← Back to main page

02.11.2026

Every great space empire starts somewhere. Ours starts on a phone, usually during a coffee break.

STARRUST is a grand space strategy game for Android. Explore the map hex by hex, claim territory, dig up hydrogen, uranium, titanium and lithium, and slowly build an empire so vast your screen will need a bigger screen. Plan your moves, play your cards, take your actions, count your points. Then count them again, because surely that can't be right.

Yes, it is a strategy game, which means you will spend twenty minutes deciding where to put one tiny ship. That ship will matter. Probably. We think. Our playtesters are still arguing about it.

We built STARRUST for people who like big decisions in short sessions: the commander who conquers a star system on the bus and gets home to discover they forgot to buy milk. The galaxy waits for no one, but it will wait until your stop.

The universe is big, old and slightly rusty. Somebody has to run it. It might as well be you.

STARRUST is currently in development. Keep an eye on this page. The stars are aligning.
